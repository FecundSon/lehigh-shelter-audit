   Main address 355 Hamilton St., Allentown, PA 18101, 610-740-5500, but

     illness), 264A S. Levan St, Allentown, 610-821-2006 — call for
     information/appointments, not walk-in emergency shelter.
   Listing any of these as a general shelter without that qualification
   would misdirect someone seeking emergency beds.

## What this report does NOT claim
- No shelter is reported as closed/defunct. Every "unverified" item is a
  gap in public web evidence, not a finding of closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a shelter is currently operating
  (see Champion Center, Horizon House, Hope Village).
- Bed availability, waitlist position, and "funds/beds available" are never
  stated; capacity figures quoted are org-described size only.
- Hours from an office/administrative page are not shelter intake hours,
  and are flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible.
- For Turning Point, and for any DV-related listing, no address is given.
  A directory address for a confidential location is a finding to report
  privately to the directory, with the address redacted in any public copy
  — it is not a correction to republish.
