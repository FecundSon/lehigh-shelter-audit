## Rules applied to this list
- No shelter, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
- No bed availability is reported as a static fact anywhere in this audit;
  intake phone/process only.
- DOMESTIC VIOLENCE: no safe-house address is published, repeated, or
  stored in these files, even if a directory lists one. DV listings record
  helpline numbers and public services only. If confidentiality is unclear,
  treat the location as confidential.
- No claim that a shelter is closed/defunct is made from a failed page load;
  such items are UNVERIFIED.
