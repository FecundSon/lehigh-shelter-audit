# Lehigh County, PA — Shelter Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-10-24. Status: published here first for statewide PA 211 and the
shelters to respond to. Nothing in this audit has been sent to statewide PA 211
or any shelter as a direct message.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 19 shelter / transitional-housing listings for
Lehigh County, PA, checked against the organisations' own websites and
other public listings. It is a curation audit, not a replacement directory:
it does not claim to be a current list of open shelters, and it should not
be used as one without checking with the shelter or PA 211 first. In
particular, it does **not** report bed availability — intake phone and
process only, because beds change day to day.

## Headline corrections (from the full audit)
1. Family Promise of Lehigh Valley — own site gives 1346 Hamilton St.,
   Allentown, PA 18102, 610-351-1368 (not 1426 Hamilton St.).
2. Sixth Street Shelter — intake is 211 referral / pre-screening only,
   no walk-ins (call 211 or 855-567-5342 Mon–Fri 9am–4pm), and its own
   FAQ now says transitional housing has ended. 219 N. 6th St., Allentown,
   610-435-1490.
3. Allentown Rescue Mission — its own site gives two addresses, 355
   Hamilton St. and 356 Court St., Allentown; directories should note
   both. Walk-in with photo ID, men 18+, Emergency Shelter 4:30pm–8am.
4. Three directory "shelters" are not general homeless shelters:
   Pathways/HOPE (housing services / case management), Terrapin House
   (IDD/mental-health tenancy support), and LVHN Transitional Living
   Center (structured mental-health residential programme, 264A S. Levan
   St., Allentown, 610-821-2006). Listing them unqualified would
   misdirect someone seeking emergency beds.
5. Allentown Warming Station — 425 South 15th St., Allentown, PA 18102,
   610-351-9622, adults 18+, from November 15, 2026, 7pm–7am, intake
   7–9pm (YMCA's own current page).
6. Allentown Halfway Home — one co-ed site at 24 S. 5th St., Allentown,
   PA 18101, 610-439-0218. A second directory address and separate
   men/women sites are third-party only.
7. Richard S. Csandl Recovery House is now TTI Allentown Recovery House,
   633 N. 4th St., Allentown, PA 18102, 610-770-9142.
8. Operation Address the Homeless (OATH) is not a shelter (support /
   warm-space programme), and its 221 N. 7th St. address is stale — its
   own Feb 2026 statement says its last day there was April 10, 2026.
   This is not a claim OATH closed; its current location is unverified.
9. "LifePath shelters" are a York County conflation — no Lehigh Valley
   LifePath homeless shelter exists; do not import York addresses into
   a Lehigh list.
10. Bethlehem county trap: Valley Youth House (539 8th Ave., Bethlehem)
    geocodes to Lehigh County, despite appearing on a Northampton-headed
    list. Victory House, Bethlehem Emergency Sheltering, and New Bethany
    geocode to Northampton County and are out of scope for a strict
    Lehigh-only list.

## Confidential location — please read
Turning Point of Lehigh Valley is a domestic-violence organisation whose
Safe House location is confidential on its own site. **No street address
for it appears in this repo.** The correct public entry is helpline only:
24/7 Helpline 610-437-3369, toll-free 877-438-4957, TTY 610-419-4594,
turningpointlv.org — call the helpline first. Any directory publishing a
street address for it is a confidential-location leak, to be reported
privately to the directory with the address redacted, never republished
as a correction.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-10-24. No shelter,
  PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- A shelter's own website is treated as authoritative over third-party
  directories. Where a shelter's own site did not state an address,
  phone, or intake route, that item is marked UNVERIFIED in the full
  report — it is a gap in public web evidence, not a finding, and must
  not be published as fact.
- **No shelter is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed or
  missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a shelter is currently operating.
- Seasonal shelters and intake rules change by year; the Warming Station
  dates above are for the season starting November 15, 2026, as stated
  on the YMCA's own current page.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from statewide PA 211 or the shelters themselves are welcome —
  that is the point of publishing it here first.
